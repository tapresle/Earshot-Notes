# Earshot 0.1.1

Released October 5, 2026. This release makes transcription of the other side of a call more reliable, adds search, and fixes several issues from 0.1.0.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.1.0: a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## New

- **Search:** search your meetings from the sidebar. Search matches meeting titles, your notes, and transcript text.
- **Log file:** the app writes a log to `~/Library/Logs/com.tedpresley.earshot/Earshot.log`, so problems leave a trail you can share. It stays on your Mac, holds at most 1 MB, and never contains transcript, notes, or summary text.
- **License notices:** the app bundles the licenses of its third-party components under `Contents/Resources/licenses`.

## Improved

- **Meeting Participants label:** the other side of a call is labeled **Meeting Participants** instead of **Them** in the transcript panel, exports, and summaries.
- **Large meeting lists:** the sidebar loads meetings a page at a time, so it opens quickly however many meetings you have.
- **Responsiveness:** database work runs in the background, so the window stays responsive while meetings load and save.
- **Contrast:** muted text now meets WCAG AA contrast in light and dark mode.

## Fixed

- **Quiet call participants:** speech from the other side of a call that was too quiet to pick up now reaches the transcript. ([#32](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/32))
- **Wrong language:** the other side of a call was sometimes transcribed as Spanish or another language. Every clip is now transcribed as English. ([#31](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/31))
- **Day labels:** **Today** and **Yesterday** in the sidebar now update when the day changes. ([#2](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/2))
- **Cancelling a stalled download:** **Cancel** now stops a model download promptly, even when the network has stalled. ([#3](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/3))
- **Not found errors:** errors now say what is missing, such as a meeting or a model, instead of always saying the meeting no longer exists. ([#4](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/4))

## Changed

- **English only:** 0.1.0 detected the language of each clip and supported English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, and Polish. Detection misread call audio, so transcription is now English only. Meetings held in other languages get an English transcript.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** on a Mac other than the one that built it, macOS blocks the first launch. Open it once, then choose **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Open Anyway**.
- **Echo without headphones:** without headphones, the other side's words can appear twice, as both **Me** and **Meeting Participants**. ([#7](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/7))
- **Switching audio output:** if you switch your Mac's audio output during a recording, such as connecting AirPods, the **Meeting Participants** side may stop transcribing until the next recording. ([#1](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/1))
- **Export all while searching:** a search with no matches hides **Export all**, and during a search **Export all** still exports every meeting, not only the results. ([#23](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/23))
- **Larger models:** memory use of the 8B and 14B models hasn't been measured during a call yet. ([#8](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/8))
