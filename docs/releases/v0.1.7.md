# Earshot Notes 0.1.7

Released October 8, 2026. Teach Earshot Notes the names your meetings use, and get more detailed summaries.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.1.0: a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## Upgrading

Open the disk image and drag **Earshot Notes** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your meetings, notes, summaries, models, settings, and permissions carry over.

The first launch updates your meetings database. After that, earlier versions of Earshot Notes can't open it, so keep 0.1.7 or later installed.

## New

- **Vocabulary:** **Settings** > **Vocabulary** lists the names your meetings use, such as products, customers, and colleagues. ([#71](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/71))
  - Transcription listens for each term from the next recording, so names it used to mishear come out spelled right.
  - Summaries spell the terms exactly as you wrote them.
  - Under **Heard as**, list what transcription writes instead, separated by commas. Saving replaces those words in every transcript, past and future, and removing them restores the original words. Search finds the corrected spelling.

## Improved

- **More detailed summaries:** summaries now aim to be notes someone who missed the meeting could act on. ([#70](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/70))
  - Topics are named after the specific thing discussed, such as a ticket, system, or customer.
  - Notes keep specifics such as ticket numbers, error messages, amounts, and dates.
  - Notes record causes, results, rejected options, and risks, and turn how-to explanations into steps.
  - Notes end with **Next steps** that give each task's owner, context, and any condition.
  - Long meetings get more room for notes.
  - To try it on a meeting you've already summarized, open the meeting and summarize it again.

## Fixed

- **Settings scrolling:** scrolling to the bottom of **Settings** no longer scrolls the whole window. ([#74](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/74))
- **Backslashes in summaries:** summaries no longer show stray backslashes, as in `\$48,000`. ([#83](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/83))

## Known limitations

- **English only:** transcription is English only. Meetings held in other languages get an English transcript.
- **Echo without headphones:** without headphones, the other side's words can appear twice, as both **Me** and **Meeting Participants**. ([#7](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/7))
- **Who said what:** everyone else on a call is one speaker, **Meeting Participants**, so summaries can credit a participant's task to the wrong person. ([#27](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/27))
- **Larger models:** memory use of the 8B and 14B models hasn't been measured during a call yet. ([#8](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/8))
