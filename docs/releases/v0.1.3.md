# Earshot 0.1.3

Released October 5, 2026. This release gives Earshot a new app icon.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.1.0: a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## Improved

- **New icon:** white sound wave arcs on a violet gradient replace the microphone icon, in Finder, the Dock, and the disk image. If the Dock or Finder still shows the old icon after you install, log out and back in to refresh the icon cache.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** on a Mac other than the one that built it, macOS blocks the first launch. Open it once, then choose **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Open Anyway**. ([#41](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/41))
- **English only:** transcription is English only. Meetings held in other languages get an English transcript.
- **Echo without headphones:** without headphones, the other side's words can appear twice, as both **Me** and **Meeting Participants**. ([#7](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/7))
- **Switching audio output:** if you switch your Mac's audio output during a recording, such as connecting AirPods, the **Meeting Participants** side may stop transcribing until the next recording. ([#1](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/1))
- **Export all while searching:** a search with no matches hides **Export all**, and during a search **Export all** still exports every meeting, not only the results. ([#23](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/23))
- **Larger models:** memory use of the 8B and 14B models hasn't been measured during a call yet. ([#8](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/8))
