# Earshot Notes 0.2.0

Released October 11, 2026. A new recommended local AI model, Qwen3.5 4B, keeps far more of a meeting's specifics in the same memory, and the list of models is shorter and stronger.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.1.0: a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## Upgrading

Open the disk image and drag **Earshot Notes** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your meetings, notes, summaries, chat, models, settings, and permissions carry over. This release doesn't change the meetings database.

If you have more than one local AI model installed, check **Settings** after upgrading: 0.2.0 stores the model choice under a new name, so it selects the model you added most recently.

## New

- **Qwen3.5 4B is the recommended local AI model:** it replaces Gemma 4 E2B. On synthetic meetings with noisy transcripts, it kept 73 of 75 planted specifics, such as decisions, numbers, and owners, where Gemma 4 E2B kept 59, and it held up in two-hour meetings. It uses the same 3.8 GB of memory and starts writing a long meeting's notes sooner. It's a 2.7 GB download. ([#96](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/96))
- **Qwen3.5 9B:** a larger option with the most accurate notes in the comparison, a 5.7 GB download that uses about 6.6 GB of memory. ([#96](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/96))

## Changed

- **A shorter list of models:** **Settings** now offers four models, all sized for a Mac with 16 GB of memory. [The model comparison](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/blob/main/docs/benchmarks/20261011-summary-models.md) has the measurements. ([#96](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/96))

  | Model | Download | Notes |
  |---|---|---|
  | Qwen3.5 4B (recommended) | 2.7 GB | Fast and light, with detailed summaries |
  | Gemma 4 E4B | 4.2 GB | Well-organized summaries, a little slower |
  | Ministral 3 8B | 5.2 GB | Very complete summaries, the slowest |
  | Qwen3.5 9B | 5.7 GB | The most detailed summaries |

- **Gemma 4 E4B uses a new file:** Google's quantization-aware file keeps more specifics than the previous one, uses 0.7 GB less memory, and downloads 0.8 GB less. A Gemma 4 E4B you downloaded before stays installed alongside the new one. Remove the old one in **Settings** to free 5.0 GB.
- **Removed from the list:** Gemma 4 E2B, Phi-4 mini, Qwen3 4B Instruct, Qwen3 8B, and Ministral 3 14B. A model you already downloaded stays installed and keeps working, without the memory limit 0.1.9 set for it. In long meetings, Ministral 3 14B can then use more memory than a 16 GB Mac can spare during a call, so switch to a listed model and remove it.
- **Ministral 3 8B's pin:** the download now points at the commit that added its file, which the model's Hugging Face page shows. The file is the same, so nothing downloads again. ([#114](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/114))

## Fixed

- **Chat citations:** a citation shared its page identity with the text before it, so React could duplicate or drop it when an answer updated. Each citation now stands on its own. ([#126](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/126))

## Known limitations

- **English only:** transcription is English only. Meetings held in other languages get an English transcript.
- **Call audio with Whistle:** the other participants' audio arrives at phone quality, so with Whistle their side of the transcript has more mistakes than yours, especially with names and technical terms. Whisper large-v3-turbo does much better.
- **Whisper and your vocabulary:** Whisper large-v3-turbo doesn't listen for your **Vocabulary** terms. **Heard as** corrections and the spellings in summaries and answers still apply.
- **Some echo remains without headphones:** echo is recognized by comparing words, so echo that transcription heard too differently from the original can still appear as **Me**. Headphones avoid it entirely. ([#88](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/88))
- **Who said what:** everyone else on a call is one speaker, **Meeting Participants**, so summaries and answers can credit a participant's task to the wrong person. ([#27](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/27))
- **Chat in long meetings:** when a meeting is too long to send to the model whole, chat sends the lines that share words with your question. A line that says the same thing in other words, or that transcription got wrong, can be missed.
