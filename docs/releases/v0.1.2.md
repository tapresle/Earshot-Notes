# Earshot 0.1.2

Released October 5, 2026. This release fixes garbled transcripts of the other side of a call when you use a Bluetooth headset.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.1.0: a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## Fixed

- **Bluetooth headsets:** with a Bluetooth headset for both your microphone and your audio, **Meeting Participants** were often missed, and what was transcribed was nonsense. The headset lowers its sample rate when its microphone opens, and Earshot kept assuming the old rate, so call audio reached the transcription model at the wrong speed. Earshot now captures at the rate the device actually runs at and follows changes during a recording. ([#36](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/36))
- **Output device:** Earshot now captures the audio device your calls play through. Before, it followed the device set for alert sounds, which can be a different one.

## Improved

- **Diagnostics:** the log file records which output device Earshot captures, its sample rate, and any rate change during a recording. The log is at `~/Library/Logs/com.tedpresley.earshot/Earshot.log`, and it never contains transcript, notes, or summary text.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** on a Mac other than the one that built it, macOS blocks the first launch. Open it once, then choose **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Open Anyway**.
- **English only:** transcription is English only. Meetings held in other languages get an English transcript.
- **Echo without headphones:** without headphones, the other side's words can appear twice, as both **Me** and **Meeting Participants**. ([#7](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/7))
- **Switching audio output:** if you switch your Mac's audio output during a recording, such as connecting AirPods, the **Meeting Participants** side may stop transcribing until the next recording. ([#1](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/1))
- **Export all while searching:** a search with no matches hides **Export all**, and during a search **Export all** still exports every meeting, not only the results. ([#23](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/23))
- **Larger models:** memory use of the 8B and 14B models hasn't been measured during a call yet. ([#8](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/8))
