# Earshot Notes 0.1.6

Released October 7, 2026. Earshot Notes is now notarized by Apple, so it opens on any Mac without a trip to **Privacy & Security**, and **Open at login** works.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.1.0: a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## Upgrading

1. Open the disk image and drag **Earshot Notes** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your meetings, notes, summaries, models, and settings carry over.
2. Open **Earshot Notes**. Because the app is now signed differently, macOS asks once more for **Microphone** and **Screen & System Audio Recording**. Allow both again. Later releases keep the same signature, so this shouldn't happen again.

If recording stays silent after you allow access, open **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security**, and turn **Earshot Notes** off and on again under **Microphone** and **Screen & System Audio Recording**.

## Improved

- **Notarized:** the app and its disk image are signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. On a Mac other than the one that built it, the app opens after the standard confirmation for apps downloaded from the internet, with no **Open Anyway** step. ([#41](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/41))

## Fixed

- **Open at login:** **Settings** > **Startup** > **Open at login** no longer says the app must be installed in Applications when it already is. ([#68](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/68))

## Known limitations

- **English only:** transcription is English only. Meetings held in other languages get an English transcript.
- **Echo without headphones:** without headphones, the other side's words can appear twice, as both **Me** and **Meeting Participants**. ([#7](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/7))
- **Brief summaries:** summaries can leave out specifics such as error messages and numbers, and can misspell names that speech recognition misheard. ([#70](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/70), [#71](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/71))
- **Larger models:** memory use of the 8B and 14B models hasn't been measured during a call yet. ([#8](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/8))
