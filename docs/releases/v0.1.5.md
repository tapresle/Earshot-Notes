# Earshot Notes 0.1.5

Released October 6, 2026. Earshot Notes now runs in the menu bar, keeps transcribing when you switch audio output during a call, and names untitled meetings from their summaries.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.1.0: a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## Upgrading

Open the disk image and drag **Earshot Notes** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your meetings, notes, summaries, models, and permissions carry over.

Closing the window no longer quits the app. To quit, choose **Quit Earshot Notes** from the menu bar icon, or press Command-Q while the window is open.

## New

- **Menu bar icon:** Earshot Notes keeps running in the menu bar after you close its window, without a Dock icon, so a recording continues in the background. The icon shows whether you're recording. Its menu starts a recording in a new meeting or stops the current one, says when only one side is being recorded and why, and opens the window. ([#29](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/29))
- **Open at login:** turn on **Settings** > **Startup** > **Open at login** to start Earshot Notes in the menu bar when you log in. ([#29](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/29))
- **Meeting titles from summaries:** an untitled meeting gets a short title from its summary, full or partial. A title you set yourself, even during the summary, is never replaced. ([#40](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/40))
- **Speech detection:** **Settings** > **Speech detection** sets how readily **My voice** and **Meeting Participants** are transcribed: **Low**, **Normal**, or **High**. Raise it if quiet speech is missed, and lower it if background noise is transcribed. Changes apply to the next recording. ([#37](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/37))
- **Keep audio recordings:** **Settings** > **Audio recordings** can keep what each recording hears, for testing and review. It's off by default. The recordings stay on your Mac, and you can delete them all from the same section. ([#50](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/50))
- **Recording consent notice:** the setup checklist and the meeting view remind you that recording laws vary, so make sure you have any consent you need. ([#30](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/30))

## Improved

- **Export all** moved to **Settings** > **Export**, where it always exports every meeting. ([#23](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/23))

## Fixed

- **Switching audio output:** switching your Mac's audio output during a recording, such as connecting AirPods, no longer stops the **Meeting Participants** transcript. Recording follows the new output, and transcript times stay in step. ([#1](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/1))
- **Phantom phrases:** short phrases such as "Thank you." no longer appear in the transcript from moments of silence, a cough, or call noise. The same words spoken clearly are kept. ([#51](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/51))

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** on a Mac other than the one that built it, macOS blocks the first launch. Open it once, then choose **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Open Anyway**. ([#41](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/41))
- **English only:** transcription is English only. Meetings held in other languages get an English transcript.
- **Echo without headphones:** without headphones, the other side's words can appear twice, as both **Me** and **Meeting Participants**. ([#7](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/7))
- **Larger models:** memory use of the 8B and 14B models hasn't been measured during a call yet. ([#8](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/8))
