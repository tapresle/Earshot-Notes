# Earshot Notes 0.1.9

Released October 10, 2026. Ask questions about a meeting and get answers that link to where they were said, choose a more accurate transcription model for calls, and get summaries of long meetings that keep their specifics.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.1.0: a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## Upgrading

Open the disk image and drag **Earshot Notes** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your meetings, notes, summaries, models, settings, and permissions carry over. This release adds a table to the meetings database for chat, so once 0.1.9 has opened it, 0.1.8 and earlier can't.

## New

- **Chat with a meeting:** each meeting has a **Chat** tab. Ask a question, such as "What did we decide about the launch date?", and your local AI model answers from the meeting's transcript and your notes. ([#24](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/24))
  - Each answer links to its sources. Select a time to scroll the transcript to that line, or **My notes** to open your notes at that passage.
  - When the meeting doesn't cover a question, the answer says so instead of guessing.
  - Conversations are saved with the meeting and included in Markdown export, in a **Chat** section between **My notes** and **Transcript**.
  - Like summaries, chat runs entirely on your Mac.
- **Whisper large-v3-turbo:** a more accurate transcription model, a 574 MB download in **Settings** > **Transcription**. It makes about half the errors, gets far more technical terms right, and does much better with the other side of a call, where call apps reduce audio to phone quality. It stays loaded while the app is open, using about 1 GB of memory. Whistle stays included and the default. [The transcription model comparison](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/blob/main/docs/benchmarks/20261009-transcription-models.md) has the measurements. Whisper runs through [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) 1.9.5, built from source. ([#25](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/25))
- **Keep the model loaded:** your local AI model now stays loaded for five minutes after a summary or answer, so the next one starts without the wait to load it. In **Settings**, choose **Never**, **15 minutes**, or **Always** instead. ([#24](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/24))
- **Software terms for your vocabulary:** **Add software terms** in **Settings** > **Vocabulary** adds 28 common technical terms, such as async, JSON, and query params, so Whistle hears them right more often. ([#104](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/104))

## Improved

- **Long meetings keep their specifics:** a meeting too long to summarize in one pass is condensed in parts, and the parts used to fill up with routine status updates. Specifics now come first, so two-hour meetings keep far more decisions, numbers, and tasks: Gemma 4 E2B kept 19 of 25 planted specifics instead of 9, and Gemma 4 E4B 21 instead of 16. Condensing is also faster. ([#95](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/95))
- **Local AI model:** **Settings**, the setup checklist, and the model download panel now call the model that writes summaries and answers questions a "local AI model".

## Known limitations

- **English only:** transcription is English only. Meetings held in other languages get an English transcript.
- **Call audio with Whistle:** the other participants' audio arrives at phone quality, so with Whistle their side of the transcript has more mistakes than yours, especially with names and technical terms. Whisper large-v3-turbo does much better.
- **Whisper and your vocabulary:** Whisper large-v3-turbo doesn't listen for your **Vocabulary** terms. **Heard as** corrections and the spellings in summaries and answers still apply.
- **Some echo remains without headphones:** echo is recognized by comparing words, so echo that transcription heard too differently from the original can still appear as **Me**. Headphones avoid it entirely. ([#88](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/88))
- **Who said what:** everyone else on a call is one speaker, **Meeting Participants**, so summaries and answers can credit a participant's task to the wrong person. ([#27](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/27))
- **Chat in long meetings:** when a meeting is too long to send to the model whole, chat sends the lines that share words with your question. A line that says the same thing in other words, or that transcription got wrong, can be missed.
