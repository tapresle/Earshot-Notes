# Earshot Notes 0.1.4

Released October 5, 2026. Earshot is now Earshot Notes, to match its website, [earshotnotes.com](https://earshotnotes.com).

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.1.0: a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## Upgrading from Earshot 0.1.3 or earlier

1. Open the disk image and drag **Earshot Notes** to **Applications**.
2. Delete the old **Earshot** app from **Applications**. The new app doesn't replace it, because the name changed. Both use the same meetings and models, so don't run them at the same time.
3. Open **Earshot Notes**. Your meetings, notes, summaries, and downloaded models are all there, and macOS keeps your microphone and system audio permissions.

If recording goes silent after the upgrade, open **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security**, and turn **Earshot Notes** off and on again under **Microphone** and **Screen & System Audio Recording**.

## Improved

- **New name:** the app, its menu, its window, and its permission prompts now say **Earshot Notes**. So do the messages that tell you where to allow access in **Privacy & Security**, so they match what **System Settings** shows.
- **Log file:** the log is now `~/Library/Logs/com.tedpresley.earshot/Earshot Notes.log`. The old `Earshot.log` in the same folder is no longer written, and you can delete it.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** on a Mac other than the one that built it, macOS blocks the first launch. Open it once, then choose **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Open Anyway**. ([#41](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/41))
- **English only:** transcription is English only. Meetings held in other languages get an English transcript.
- **Echo without headphones:** without headphones, the other side's words can appear twice, as both **Me** and **Meeting Participants**. ([#7](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/7))
- **Switching audio output:** if you switch your Mac's audio output during a recording, such as connecting AirPods, the **Meeting Participants** side may stop transcribing until the next recording. ([#1](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/1))
- **Export all while searching:** a search with no matches hides **Export all**, and during a search **Export all** still exports every meeting, not only the results. ([#23](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/23))
- **Larger models:** memory use of the 8B and 14B models hasn't been measured during a call yet. ([#8](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot/issues/8))
