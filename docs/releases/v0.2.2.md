# Earshot Notes 0.2.2

Released October 15, 2026. Two new **Recording** settings can start recording when you create a meeting and summarize it when you stop, and the meeting list has its own right-click menu for exporting and deleting.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.1.0: a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## Upgrading

Open the disk image and drag **Earshot Notes** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your meetings, notes, summaries, chat, models, settings, and permissions carry over. This release doesn't change the meetings database.

## New

- **Start recording when you create a meeting:** turn on **Start recording when I create a new meeting** in **Settings** > **Recording**, and **New meeting** starts recording right away. If another meeting is already recording, it keeps recording instead. The setting is off until you turn it on. ([#141](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/141))
- **Summarize when you stop recording:** turn on **Summarize a meeting when I stop recording** in **Settings** > **Recording**, and the local AI model writes the summary once the last words are transcribed. The meeting switches to its **Summary** tab so you can watch it appear, or to see why there was nothing to summarize. A meeting that already has a summary keeps it, and a summary waits for a chat answer in progress to finish. The setting is off until you turn it on. ([#142](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/142), [#158](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/158))
- **Meeting list menu:** right-click a meeting, or select it and press Shift+F10, for **Export** and **Delete**. With several meetings selected, the menu offers **Export N meetings** to a folder you choose, and doesn't offer to delete them. ([#140](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/140))

## Improved

- **Deleting asks in a dialog:** **Delete**, from the menu or the meeting's header, now asks for confirmation in a dialog instead of inline. The header's **Delete** is red, like every other delete in the app. ([#140](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/140), [#156](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/156))
- **Settings use the whole window:** the transcript pane hides while **Settings** is open, so the settings have room beside the meeting list. ([#143](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/143))

## Known limitations

- **English only:** transcription is English only. Meetings held in other languages get an English transcript.
- **Call audio with Whistle:** the other participants' audio arrives at phone quality, so with Whistle their side of the transcript has more mistakes than yours, especially with names and technical terms. Whisper large-v3-turbo does much better.
- **Whisper and your vocabulary:** Whisper large-v3-turbo doesn't listen for your **Vocabulary** terms. **Heard as** corrections and the spellings in summaries and answers still apply.
- **Some echo remains without headphones:** echo is recognized by comparing words, so echo that transcription heard too differently from the original can still appear as **Me**. Headphones avoid it entirely. ([#88](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/88))
- **Who said what:** everyone else on a call is one speaker, **Meeting Participants**, so summaries and answers can credit a participant's task to the wrong person. ([#27](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/27))
- **Chat in long meetings:** when a meeting is too long to send to the model whole, chat sends the lines that share words with your question. A line that says the same thing in other words, or that transcription got wrong, can be missed.
