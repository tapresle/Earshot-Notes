# Earshot 0.1.0

Released October 4, 2026. This is the first release.

Earshot is a meeting notes app that runs entirely on your Mac. It transcribes both sides of your calls as they happen, gives you a notepad during the meeting, and turns your notes and the transcript into meeting notes with a local language model. Nothing leaves your computer. The only network request is a model download that you start yourself.

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple Silicon.
- macOS 26 or later.
- About 30 MB for the app, plus 2.5 to 8.2 GB for each summary model you download.

## Getting started

- **First-run checklist:** walks you through allowing the microphone and system audio, and suggests a summary model. You can open it again from **Settings**.
- **Permissions:** the checklist asks for microphone and system audio access directly, and links to the right **System Settings** pane if you declined.

## Meetings and notes

- **New meeting:** creates a meeting and opens it, ready to record.
- **Meeting list:** the sidebar groups meetings by day, with **Today** and **Yesterday** labels.
- **Titles:** rename a meeting by editing its title.
- **Notepad:** the **My notes** tab saves as you type. Your notes guide the summary, so the points you write down come first.
- **Delete:** delete a meeting and its notes, after a confirmation.

## Live transcription

- **Two sides of the call:** transcribes your microphone as **Me** and your Mac's audio, such as a video call, as **Them**.
- **Live panel:** speech appears in the transcript panel a few seconds after it's spoken, with a timestamp for each line.
- **On-device:** uses Cactus Whistle, bundled with the app, so transcription works offline from the first launch. It supports English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, and Polish.
- **Long meetings:** transcription continues for the whole meeting, cutting the audio at pauses in speech.
- **Partial access:** if system audio access is off, recording continues with your microphone alone and explains how to turn system audio on.
- **Headphones hint:** suggests headphones, because without them your microphone also picks up the other side of the call.

## Summaries

- **Summarize so far:** summarizes a meeting while it's still recording. The summary is marked as partial.
- **Summarize:** after the meeting, writes the full summary from your notes and the whole transcript.
- **Streaming:** the summary appears as it's written, and you can cancel it.
- **Long meetings:** a meeting too long for the model to read at once is condensed in parts first.
- **Local models:** summaries run on your Mac through llama.cpp, using its GPU.

## Summary models

- **Seven models to choose from**, recommended first, then smallest to largest:

  | Model | Size | Notes |
  |---|---|---|
  | Gemma 4 E2B (recommended) | 3.1 GB | Fast and light. A good fit for most meetings. |
  | Phi-4 mini | 2.5 GB | Small and fast, with shorter summaries. |
  | Qwen3 4B Instruct | 2.5 GB | Small, with detailed summaries for its size. |
  | Gemma 4 E4B | 5.0 GB | Better summaries than E2B, a little slower. |
  | Qwen3 8B | 5.0 GB | High-quality summaries. Slower, and uses more memory. |
  | Ministral 3 8B | 5.2 GB | High-quality summaries. Slower, and uses more memory. |
  | Ministral 3 14B | 8.2 GB | The most capable option. Needs at least 16 GB of memory. |

- **Verified downloads:** each model is pinned to an exact file on Hugging Face and checked against its SHA-256 checksum before use. Interrupted downloads resume where they stopped, and you can cancel a download.
- **Model source:** each model shows its Hugging Face repository, quantization, and revision, with a link to its page.
- **Import:** use any GGUF model file you already have.
- **Choose a model:** pick the model summaries use in **Settings**. Downloading or importing another model keeps your current choice; only your first model is selected automatically.
- **Remove:** delete a model you no longer need to free disk space, after a confirmation.

## Export

- **Markdown files:** export one meeting, a selection of meetings, or all of them. Select several meetings in the sidebar with Command-click or Shift-click.
- **File layout:** each file has the summary first, then your notes, then the transcript, so every export has the same shape.
- **Naming:** files are named by date and title, such as `2026-10-04-design-sync.md`. Multi-meeting exports go to a folder you choose, and the app reports any files it couldn't write.

## Settings

- **Microphone:** choose which microphone to record from, or use the system default. The app warns you when the chosen microphone isn't connected.
- **Summary model:** choose, add, and remove summary models.
- **Transcription:** shows the transcription model in use.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** on a Mac other than the one that built it, macOS blocks the first launch. Open it once, then choose **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Open Anyway**.
- **Echo without headphones:** without headphones, the other side's words can appear twice, as both **Me** and **Them**.
- **Switching audio output:** if you switch your Mac's audio output during a recording, such as connecting AirPods, the **Them** side may stop transcribing until the next recording.
- **Day labels:** **Today** and **Yesterday** in the sidebar don't update if the app stays open past midnight.
- **Cancelling a stalled download:** if the network stalls, **Cancel** takes effect only once the connection responds or times out.
- **Larger models:** memory use of the 8B and 14B models hasn't been measured during a call yet.
