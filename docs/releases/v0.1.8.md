# Earshot Notes 0.1.8

Released October 8, 2026. More accurate transcripts of real conversations, much less echo when you don't wear headphones, and summaries that call you "you".

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.1.0: a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

## Upgrading

Open the disk image and drag **Earshot Notes** to **Applications**, replacing the old copy. Your meetings, notes, summaries, models, settings, and permissions carry over. This release doesn't change the meetings database, so 0.1.7 can still open it.

## Improved

- **Whole sentences in the transcript:** Earshot Notes now waits for a 2-second pause before it transcribes, instead of half a second. People pause mid-sentence, and transcription understood the pieces much worse than the whole: short words and technical terms came out as everyday words, such as "async" as "a sink". Transcript lines now read as whole sentences, and terms in your **Vocabulary** are recognized more often. Each line appears about 2 seconds after the speaker stops. ([#99](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/99))
- **Echo without headphones:** without headphones, your microphone also picks up the other side of the call from your speakers. ([#7](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/7), [#99](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/99))
  - A **Me** line that repeats what the other participants just said is removed, even if it was already shown.
  - When you talk over the echo, the repeated words are removed from your line, and your own words stay.
- **Summaries call you "you":** notes no longer use **Me** as a name, as in "Me suggested a fix" or "(Me)" as a task's owner. They say "You suggested a fix" and "(you)". ([#100](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/100))
- **Sidebar:** **New meeting**, search, and **Settings** stay in place while the meeting list scrolls. ([#86](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/86))
- **Transcription engine:** the needle engine that runs Whistle is updated to its latest production build. Transcripts are the same, at the same speed. ([#64](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/64))

## Known limitations

- **English only:** transcription is English only. Meetings held in other languages get an English transcript.
- **Call audio:** the other participants' audio arrives at phone quality, so their side of the transcript has more mistakes than yours, especially with names and technical terms. ([#25](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/25))
- **Some echo remains without headphones:** echo is recognized by comparing words, so echo that transcription heard too differently from the original can still appear as **Me**. Headphones avoid it entirely. ([#88](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/88))
- **Who said what:** everyone else on a call is one speaker, **Meeting Participants**, so summaries can credit a participant's task to the wrong person. ([#27](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/27))
- **Long meetings:** a meeting too long to summarize in one pass is condensed in parts, and details can get lost when most of it is routine updates. ([#95](https://github.com/tapresle/Earshot-Notes/issues/95))
